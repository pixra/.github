### 🏢 About Pixra Global

> **Building the ecosystems of tomorrow, today.**

Pixra Global is an emerging tech organization dedicated to engineering scalable B2B and B2C products, complete digital ecosystems, and next-generation solutions. We operate at the intersection of modern web architecture, advanced systems engineering (C++), and intelligent design.

**Our Vision:** To architect high-performance, resilient systems and eventually transition into deep-tech research focusing on climate-resilient, intelligent Humanoids that assist humanity.

<br>

### 🚀 Our Flagship Product

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://qudu.in"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/QuduHub-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=vercel&logoColor=ffffff&border=ffffff" alt="QuduHub Website" /></a>
</p>

**[QuduHub](https://qudu.in)** is our next-generation education management platform. It empowers institutions to seamlessly manage universities, courses, subjects, and study resources (notes, PDFs, PYQs).
*   **Tech Stack:** Built with modern, edge-ready technologies including **Next.js**, **Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2)**, and **Supabase**.
*   **Repositories:** 
    *   [`admin-frontend`](https://github.com/Radhebharadwaj/admin-frontend) - The admin dashboard UI.
    *   [`admin-backend`](https://github.com/Radhebharadwaj/admin-backend) - The robust, scalable backend infrastructure.
